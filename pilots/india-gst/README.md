# India GST pilot

India is the first implementation adapter, not the template for the global
model. The pilot tests two different failure modes before any broad backfill.

## Track 1: measurable acquisition coverage

Select one official, enumerable central GST publication series and one closed
date range. Account for every item in the official index, publish every gap,
preserve source bytes outside ordinary Git, and reproduce each Markdown
conversion from its artifact hash.

## Track 2: end-to-end legal trace

Select one GST provision family and trace it across the Act, Rules,
notifications, circulars or instructions, commencement and effective dates,
and relevant judicial treatment. Every relationship must cite an official
artifact passage and carry a review state.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every item declared by the Track 1 index is captured or listed as a known gap.
- Re-running extraction produces identical Markdown from identical bytes.
- Publication, legal effect, facts period, authority cutoff, and corpus snapshot
  remain distinguishable.
- The evidence bundle exposes contrary authority and insufficient coverage.
- A fixture correction invalidates and rebuilds every affected derived record.
- No report or fixture contains a user's facts or conversation.

The exact source series, date range, and provision family are selected only
after the source register and rights/privacy review are complete.
