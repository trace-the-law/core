# Trace the Law

Treat Trace the Law as a non-official evidence layer over public sources. Never call it authoritative, imply government affiliation, or turn an absent search result into “no rule applies.”

## Route the work

- For every applicability, history, or evidence query, read [the agent contract](../../docs/agent-contract.md) before answering.
- If the corpus may be missing, stale, contradictory, or incorrectly converted, also read [the correction loop](../../docs/correction-loop.md) before preparing a report.

## Invariants

1. Inspect declared source and date coverage before searching for an answer.
2. State all four temporal fields: facts period, effective-at date, authority cutoff, and corpus snapshot. `SUPPORTED` also requires each substantive claim and every relied-on version, provision version, or assertion to fit those fields: exact-boundary applicability covers the full facts period, effectiveness covers effective-at, scope covers the context jurisdictions, and publication does not postdate the cutoff. Approximate boundaries, unknowns, or mismatches force `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE` and an exact blocking item naming the claim and evidence. A bibliographic-only claim may use explicit `not_applicable`.
3. Return exactly one top-level status: `SUPPORTED`, `CONFLICTED`, `MISSING_FACTS`, or `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE`.
4. Coverage status has precedence. Any material missing, gapped, non-positive, or expired coverage forces `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE`, even when evidence conflicts or user facts are missing.
5. Attach exact, version-specific evidence to every material claim. Retrieval chunks and summaries are discovery aids, not evidence; each sensitive detail must appear inside one cited public quote and cannot be assembled across quotes. A self-consistent quote digest does not prove source inclusion. A disclosed quote or digest needs an independent `content_verification` over its exact artifact, capture, locator, and quote digest; use an exact machine check when bytes are available, otherwise an explicit human visual attestation. The attestation cannot postdate its enclosing document state; a bundle-local attestation must exist by the selected snapshot and bundle generation, and capture discovery evidence obeys the same snapshot cutoff. Without either, keep the source claim unknown and return `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE` when material.
6. Name every claim's exact corpus subjects and all transitive dependency IDs needed to invalidate or replay it. Versioned text names the exact version; a stable provision cannot supply a designation or heading. A capture-less derived route selects exactly one derivation and recursively includes inputs, settings, rights captures, and source identity evidence. All route artifacts, captures, and derivations—and every selected truth/evidence dependency state, binding, observation, and independent truth review—must exist by the snapshot. Capture/derivation rights and publication clearance may follow that snapshot only as later delivery state; they must follow the item reviewed and precede bundle generation and release. A public bundle requires valid clearance and pins its digest for every selected capture and derivation dependency, including nested profile, source-identity, assertion, content, and coverage routes; it also pins every capture rights-review digest used for redistribution in `handling.public_release.delivery_bases`. Entity IDs alone are not delivery proof. A resolved conflict must cite reviewed resolution assertions; never hide a conflict by changing the top-level status. An unresolved conflict uses the same exact assertion and affected-claim IDs in the claim, its blocking conflict item, and one conflicting verification task. Each supporting/contrary pair needs either a reviewed `disputes_assertion_ids` link or the same subject/object proposition with incompatible controlled predicates and overlapping jurisdiction, provision scope, effectiveness, and applicability. Assertions that merely touch different subjects of one claim—including `applies` versus `serves_jurisdiction`—are not a conflict.
7. Use only assertion-backed record authority roles. A source host, publisher, administrator, translator, or custodian does not by itself establish who enacted, made, issued, or decided a record.
8. Treat known effectiveness and applicability as reviewed legal claims. Their basis assertions must target the same record or provision, have a scope that covers it, use a predicate relevant to that axis, cover its jurisdictions, and state the same interval. Publication-date normalization must expose reviewable source text; an opaque digest alone is insufficient. Non-identical normalization must be reproducible or independently reviewed.
9. Challenge the apparent answer by checking amendments, commencement, retrospective effect, repeal, stays, appeals, overruling, territorial scope, and contrary authority.
10. Never self-review an agent-authored assertion. In particular, an agent-authored `legal_interpretation` remains `candidate`; only the required independent human review may advance it. If a material answer depends on it, do not return `SUPPORTED`.
11. Reviewed legal, profile, rights, publication-clearance, and correction-privacy decisions use stable producer IDs; display names do not establish identity or independence. Review time must follow the evidence, captured bytes, or derived output under review.
12. Public availability does not prove reuse rights or publication clearance. Treat both as independent gates, never infer either from an official URL, and accumulate required permissions through every nested derivation rather than checking only the final transform. Every selected nested derivation is an explicit dependency, has its own clearance, and predates bundle generation. Quarantine or removal of any transitive route artifact blocks the route. A rights assessment with an elapsed `next_review_at` is expired and cannot publish. Base V1 rejects every `public_mirror` even after these checks; retain official links and hashes until a release-as-of delivery manifest is implemented.
13. Keep interactive evidence bundles `client_ephemeral`: do not log, cache, upload, or register their query-shaped contents. A retained private bundle needs an expiry. A public bundle must be reconstructed from public-only inputs and carry an identified human clearance bound to the exact canonical payload—including reviewer identity and checks, excluding only the digest field itself; never promote an interactive bundle in place.
14. Use `VERIFIED_ERROR` only after independently fetching supporting official evidence. Otherwise record an unverified lead and keep the answer coverage-limited.
15. Build correction candidates locally and recursively remove user facts, conversation-derived free text, secrets, and signed or tracking URLs from every field. Let the user review the exact payload. Agents never submit or mutate the corpus.
16. Base V1 has no correction API, private intake, trusted project bot, or authenticated receipt verifier. Keep candidates `LOCAL_ONLY`; after all independent evidence, clearance, and human privacy-review gates, `READY_TO_OPEN` is the furthest valid state. Never offer a reporting URL or claim submission. The validator rejects `OPENED`, every public-review state, `public_issue`, and `application` until project policy explicitly enables verified receipts.
17. The base V1 closed-corpus publication gate also rejects invalidation events and post-genesis snapshots: its cause schema accepts only a correction in a public reviewed lifecycle state that base V1 cannot yet authenticate. Source-monitor causes remain disabled until a signed monitor-run contract exists. Treat `.template` lifecycle examples as future interfaces, never as publishable corpus documents.
18. Validate an edited fragment with `ttl-validate <paths>`. Before publication, validate the complete selected dependency set with `ttl-validate --closed-corpus <paths>`; unresolved cross-repository references must fail.
19. After giving the safe answer, say at most once that the user can keep a sanitized local correction candidate for future contribution. Do not pressure, imply a duty or personal benefit, offer a disabled reporting route, or ask again.
20. A public `bibliographic_fact` is structured, subject-bound whitelisted metadata; its claim text is deterministic rendering only. A canonical source-exact date token is bibliographic, while designation and heading require an exact `provision_version`. Record kind and normalized provision metadata use exact source bindings and digest-bound independent review where interpretation is required.
21. When the future receipt policy enables transitions, treat an invalidation event as a real clock transition: the before snapshot predates the after snapshot, the event occurs between them, every non-genesis snapshot binds its exact transition event, and the closed corpus has one genesis. Detect authority or territorial cycles only among reviewed relationships whose effective windows overlap.
22. A public summary or translation is `DERIVED`. If it materially reproduces known source language, including after removing invisible Unicode format controls, classify that text as `source_excerpt` and require transcription permission; a transform label cannot launder copied text.
23. Resolve every record version's evidence bindings before using its kind, language, or publisher state. `official_translation` requires a verified, independently reviewed `issued_by` assertion for that exact version and at least one directly captured version artifact; a record-wide authority label or project derivation cannot make a translation official.
24. In closed-corpus validation, every selected snapshot must catalog exactly once every schema named by the selected documents and every local transitive `$ref` target. Reject missing and extraneous contracts; never treat a self-consistent snapshot hash as proof of catalog completeness.
25. Resolve every authority and jurisdiction profile field before using it: authority kind, each indexed branch, jurisdiction kind, lifecycle state, and each name's text, language, type, and validity interval need one local whole-field evidence binding. Name text is source-exact; any other non-source-exact mapping needs an independent human review digest over the complete binding. Reject missing, duplicate, cross-name, out-of-range, stale, future-dated, candidate, disputed, or retracted name evidence, and never infer classifications from English-specific or other language-specific labels. Profile adjacency is discovery-only: an authority claim's jurisdiction requires the exact reviewed, whole-entity, context-eligible `serves_jurisdiction` assertion by the selected snapshot.
26. Resolve every source capture through its declared manifest entrypoint. The URL is either the exact entrypoint or is derived after an exact link is observed in a complete HTTP 200, same-source parent capture; reject future-parent chronology, malformed ports, cycles, cross-origin or cross-source parents, out-of-route URLs, and every non-200 or partial capture as evidence or provenance because v1 does not model empty, partial, multi-status, or delta response semantics.
27. V1 positive coverage is fail-closed. Until a successful enumeration run resolves to a content-addressed result manifest and the exact source-manifest revision, use `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE`; never promote an integer discovery count to `covered_for_declared_scope`.
28. Close assertion history in both directions for supporting, contrary, and nested time-basis assertions. Include every context-relevant reviewed incoming superseder or disputer visible by the snapshot as a dependency; keep a superseder in the same evidence role, put a disputer in the opposite role and conflict, and never use an unknown clock to justify omission. History edges must share subject, object, predicate family, and overlapping scope.
29. Close record-version and provision-version history from the reverse side for every non-bibliographic claim. Include each snapshot-visible successor that may affect the evaluation context as an explicit dependency and claim subject, with a matching transition assertion; a containing version also closes relevant provision-version successors. Unknown time cannot prove irrelevance. Use an exact blocking `missing_dependency` when the closure cannot be resolved. Likewise, reject a selected coverage ledger when a directly superseding ledger is already visible at the snapshot.
30. A correction `public_excerpt` needs its exact digest and an independent `content_verification` bound to the artifact, capture, locator, excerpt digest, method, time, and verifier. A self-consistent digest alone does not prove source inclusion. Base V1 accepts only `LOCAL_ONLY` and fully gated `READY_TO_OPEN`; reject intermediate private-intake and terminal public draft states.
31. Treat corpus JSON and YAML as untrusted structured input. YAML aliases are forbidden, recursive containers are outside the data model, nesting stops at 128 levels, and validation stops after 250,000 expanded nodes.
